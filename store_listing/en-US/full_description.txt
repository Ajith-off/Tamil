Teach your child to read and write Tamil, one letter at a time.

அஆஇஈ தமிழ் எழுத்து is a Tamil-first learning app for children (ages roughly 4-8) that covers all 247 characters of the Tamil alphabet — the 12 vowels plus the ஃ aaytham, 18 consonants, and all 216 vowel-consonant combinations. A further 6 characters for sounds Tamil has borrowed are taught afterward as their own set. Every letter is traced by hand on screen, guided by real stroke-order data, not a shape guessed from a font.

WRITE EVERY LETTER, THE RIGHT WAY
Each character is traced across four levels that ease your child from guided to independent:
• Easy — a full guide tube with a moving waypoint showing exactly where to go next
• Medium — the same guide, without the waypoint
• Hard — nothing on screen but a silent fluency bonus for a smooth, confident hand
• Challenge — a blank freehand exam, scored on how closely the shape matches

Stroke order isn't guessed — a font only knows what a letter looks like, not what a hand draws first. Each character's writing order comes from a dedicated stroke-order reference, checked by hand.

LEARN THROUGH PLAY
Word Builder turns 31 base characters into real Tamil words. Kids drag letters together to build words like ஆடு (goat) and ஆமை (turtle), watching two letters join into the character they make — the same lesson as tracing, from a different angle.

TRACK PROGRESS, THE HONEST WAY
Stars, coins and a printable certificate track what your child has learned. Nothing is ever locked — every letter and every game is open from the very first launch. A gentle guide ring, not a padlock, shows where to go next.

BUILT SAFE FOR CHILDREN, FROM THE GROUND UP
• No login, no account, no chat, no social sharing — ever
• No analytics of any kind
• All progress and drawings stay on the device
• A parental gate (a simple sum) stands in front of every purchase, ad setting and outbound link
• Optional rewarded ads appear only inside the parent-only zone — never on a screen your child sees, and never blocking a letter

BILINGUAL BY DESIGN
The whole app works in Tamil or English, switchable any time from the parent zone. Tamil comes first, because that's what this app is for.

Whether your child is just starting to recognize அ, ஆ, இ or is ready to write the full 216-letter உயிர்மெய் table, this app grows with them — one honestly-scored letter at a time.
